Privacy

Last updated: 2026-08-02

What Hermiona is

Hermiona is a premium photo editor that runs entirely in your browser. Open a photo, adjust light and color, apply film looks and camera character, optionally run on-device portrait optics — then export. There is no account, and no Hermiona app backend.

Your photos

Photos are never uploaded to Hermiona servers. There is no Hermiona image API. Processing happens on your device, in the browser tab (Canvas / CPU or GPU where available).

When you download an edit, the file is written by your browser to your device. Closing the tab discards the in-memory edit session unless you export first.

On-device AI models

Portrait / Analyze features can use optional on-device machine learning (MediaPipe Tasks Vision and related assets). On first use of those features, your browser may download model and WASM files from Google / CDN hosts (for example jsDelivr and Google Cloud Storage) to your device.

  • Inference runs locally; image pixels are not sent to Hermiona.
  • Those downloads are third-party infrastructure (CDN / model hosting), not Hermiona servers.
  • Core editing (light, color, film, age, crop, export) does not require those models.

Storage

Hermiona keeps the current session in memory. The browser may cache app shell assets and, after first use of Portrait/Analyze, cached model/WASM files in its normal HTTP cache.

There are no Hermiona cloud drafts. Future local-only drafts (if added) would stay on your device; this page would be updated if that changes.

Analytics & tracking

None currently. Hermiona does not ship third-party analytics, ad pixels, or session trackers. We do not collect usage telemetry through the app as shipped.
